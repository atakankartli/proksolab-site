Small experiments, honest numbers.

Proksolab is a science project. We run simple experiments on soil, water, air and energy, and we publish the data and the method so anyone can repeat them.

What we study

Everyday questions about the environment, tested with cheap sensors, careful notes and a lot of patience.

🌱

Soil

Moisture, compost and how different mixes change how fast plants grow.

💧

Water

Rainwater collection, filtering and how clean the result really is.

🌬️

Air

Indoor CO₂ and particles, and what opening a window actually does.

☀️

Energy

Small solar panels, batteries and how much power a sunny day gives.

Every number has a label

Each figure we publish says where it came from, so you know which ones you can rely on.

MEASURED

Read directly from a sensor or instrument, with the raw data attached.

CALCULATED

Worked out from measured values, with the formula shown.

ESTIMATED

Our best guess. We flag these clearly and never present one as a result.

How an experiment runs

The same four steps every time, written down before we start.

  1. Question

    One simple thing we want to know, written as plainly as we can.

  2. Prediction

    What we expect to happen and why, recorded before any data comes in.

  3. Measure

    Repeated readings, with controls and notes on anything that went wrong.

  4. Share

    Data, method and a short write-up, including results we did not expect.

What we publish

Things you can open, check and repeat yourself.

  • Raw data as plain CSV files.
  • Method notes detailed enough to repeat the experiment.
  • Parts lists for every sensor and setup we use.
  • Analysis code that turns the data into the charts.
  • Photos of the setup as it was actually built.
  • A short write-up where every number carries its label.

Questions

Can I repeat an experiment at home?

That is the idea. Every write-up comes with a parts list and method notes so you can try it yourself.

Is the data free to use?

Yes. Use it, check it, and tell us if you find a mistake.

Can I suggest an experiment?

Please do. Send us your question and we will tell you if we can test it.

Send us an idea, a result or a correction.
