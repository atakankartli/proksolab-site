What the tools do

An assistant that handles the tedious parts of RF simulation, so engineers spend their time on decisions.

📐

Model from your files

Reads KiCad projects and gerbers, rebuilds the stackup and geometry, and flags every value it had to assume.

⚡

Drive the solver

Sets up ports, boundaries and sweeps in HFSS or openEMS, runs them, and pulls back S-parameters and 3D patterns.

🔍

Check the layout

Design-rule and manufacturability checks on RF boards, including BGA fan-out and controlled-impedance feeds.

📊

Write the report

Plots, tables and a written summary, with every number labelled as measured, solved or estimated.
