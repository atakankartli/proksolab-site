Empty rooms still burn energy

Most buildings decide whether a room is in use with tools that were never built to answer that question.

🙋

Motion sensors miss still people

A standard motion sensor only sees movement. Someone reading or typing quietly gets plunged into darkness, so people tape over the sensor or set long timeouts.

⏱️

Timers just guess

Schedules heat and light rooms by the clock, not by who is there. Every cancelled meeting and early finish is paid for anyway.

📷

Cameras cost privacy

Cameras can count people, but nobody wants to be filmed in a meeting room, a classroom or at home.

How it works

One small sensor on the ceiling or wall. It answers a single question, all the time: is anyone here?

  1. Sense

    The sensor picks up the tiny movements a person makes just by breathing, so a still person still counts as present.

  2. Decide on the device

    Processing happens inside the sensor. It sends out a yes or no, never images or recordings.

  3. Act

    Lights, heating, cooling and ventilation follow the real state of the room, through a relay or your building system.

Questions

Does it record video or audio?

No. The sensor has no camera or microphone. It only reports whether someone is present.

Will the lights turn off while I am sitting still?

That is the problem we solve. The sensor detects the small movements of breathing, so a still person keeps the room on.

Does it work with our building system?

It can switch a relay directly or report to a building management system. Tell us what you run and we will confirm.

How much will we save?

It depends on how often your rooms are empty. Use the calculator above for an estimate, then run a pilot to measure it.
