What we do

Hardware engineering for teams building radar and RF products. We can handle one piece of the design or the whole chain.

01

Antenna design

Series-fed patch arrays, MIMO TX/RX layouts and radome tuning for 60 GHz radar chips.

02

EM simulation

Full-wave HFSS and openEMS models: S-parameters, 3D patterns and package and mold sensitivity sweeps.

03

RF PCB layout

KiCad boards with BGA fan-out, controlled-impedance feeds and design-for-manufacturing review before fab.

04

Radar system analysis

Link budgets, coverage maps and waveform checks, so you know the minimum detectable target before you commit.

Every number has a label

Each figure in our reports says where it came from, so you know which ones you can rely on.

MEASURED

Read directly from your gerbers, drawings or bench data.

SOLVED

Computed by a full-wave field solver on a model we can hand you.

ESTIMATED

Engineering judgement. We flag these clearly, and we never quote one as a result.

How a project runs

Short loops, written results, and no surprises when the boards come back.

  1. Send what you have

    Gerbers, stackup, chip datasheet, or just the requirements on a page.

  2. Scope

    We say what we will model, what we will measure from your files, and what stays an estimate.

  3. Design & solve

    Layout changes and full-wave runs, with checkpoints so you can steer early.

  4. Hand-off

    A report, the models behind it, and fab-ready files if we touched the board.

What you get

Things your team can open, rerun and check, not just slides.

  • S-parameters as Touchstone files for every port we simulate.
  • 3D radiation patterns with gain, beamwidth and sidelobe numbers.
  • Simulation projects you can rerun after we are done.
  • KiCad files with DFM notes, ready to send to fab.
  • Link budget showing range against target size.
  • A written report where every number carries its label.

Questions

Can you work from gerbers alone?

Yes. We rebuild the stackup and geometry from the files and flag anything we had to assume, such as material values.

Do we keep the simulation models?

Yes. The models are part of the hand-off, so your team can rerun them or extend them later.

Can you take on just one part of the design?

Yes. Many projects are a single antenna review or one simulation sweep. We can also run the whole chain from antenna to link budget.

Our board already failed. Can you help?

Send the files and what you saw on the bench. Debugging a board that has already been built is a good place to start.

Have a board that needs to see further?

Send us your gerbers or your requirements. We reply within one working day.
